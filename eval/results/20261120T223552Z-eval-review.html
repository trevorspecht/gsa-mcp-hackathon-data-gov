Why these do not demonstrate correlation

  • Geography differs: Maryland statewide climate cannot be directly paired with practices across the entire Chesapeake watershed.
  • Practice observations are sparse: cover crops have only two observation years and tillage four; missing years cannot be filled in as observations.
  • Endpoint differences are not trends. The temperature comparison above is not an estimated warming trend, and annual precipitation totals do not describe seasonal timing or extremes.
  • Area is not adoption rate: practice figures are spatially allocated estimates, not direct measurements at every catchment or percentages of farms adopting a practice. Cover-crop and tillage areas can overlap.
  • These practices were reported for soil and water-quality management, not demonstrated climate-driven adoption. Even a valid correlation would not establish causation.
