Why this does not establish a climate–practice correlation

The available reviewed records do not align sufficiently:

  • Different geography: NOAA temperature and precipitation records cover Maryland statewide; agricultural estimates cover the entire multistate Chesapeake watershed.
  • Sparse agricultural observations: Conservation tillage is available for 1985, 1992, 2002 and 2012; cover crops only for 2002 and 2012.
  • Different temporal detail: The reviewed NOAA series are annual records for 1985–2012. Annual precipitation totals do not capture seasonal timing or extremes.
  • Attribution is unresolved: The agricultural records describe soil- and water-quality management practices; they do not demonstrate that implementation changed in response to climate.

Consequently, I cannot report a defensible correlation coefficient, its direction, or a climate-driven effect from these records.

The concrete next step is to find climate and practice observations for the same counties or subwatersheds over matching years, rather than compare Maryland averages with watershed totals.
